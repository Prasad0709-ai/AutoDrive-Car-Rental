Terms of Service & Rental Policies

Last Updated: October 2026 • Task ID: JV-BN-002

All primary and secondary drivers must be at least 21 years of age and hold a valid, non-expired driver's license held for at least one full calendar year. Renters under age 25 may be subject to a young driver surcharge on selected high-performance categories.

A refundable security deposit is authorized upon booking confirmation based on the vehicle category (Economy: $150, Compact: $150-200, SUV: $250-350, Luxury: $500-600). The deposit is fully released within 3-5 business days following return vehicle inspection with no damages or fuel deficits.

Bookings in PENDING or CONFIRMED status may be cancelled free of charge prior to scheduled pickup date directly through the customer dashboard. Completed or currently active rentals are non-cancellable. Upon valid cancellation, any paid amounts are marked as REFUNDED.

All combustion and hybrid vehicles are dispatched with a full tank of fuel and should be returned with the equivalent fuel level. Electric vehicles are delivered with at least 80% charge and can be returned with 20% or higher.
